# A soft semi-transparent smoke/steam puff (airbrush over a translucent core).
NewDocument(12, 12)
SelectTool(Ellipse); SetShapeFill(true); SetPrimaryColor(#B8BEC855)
Stroke([(2,2),(9,9)])
SelectTool(Airbrush); SetBrushSize(5); SetIntensity(140); SetSeed(5); SetPrimaryColor(#9AA0AA88)
Tap(6,6); Tap(4,7); Tap(8,5); Tap(6,4)
