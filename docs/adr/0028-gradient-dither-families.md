# The Gradient's dither is a family of per-pixel threshold matrices, not only Bayer

**Decided and implemented 2026-09-06.** Engine: `GradientSpec.dither` becomes a `DitherKind`
(`Off · Bayer 2/4/8 · BlueNoise · Halftone 4/8 · HLines 2/4/8 · VLines 2/4/8 · Diagonal 4/8 ·
WhiteNoise · Ign`), the verb `SetGradientDither(token)` accepts the new families as words while the
Bayer sizes stay the bare numbers they have carried since ADR 0025, and the `assert.gradient`
oracle now compares the canvas rect only (it used to fail on every document's overscan gutter).
Shell: the Dither page lists the families by section, each previewed as a full-width OFF→ON ramp
strip that walks every density step (since 2026-09-16; 50 % squares before), and the row-1 swatch
previews the family in force at 50 %. Generated threshold tables (`tools/dither/gen_tables.py`)
ship in both the engine and the shell from one run. No `.mkpx`, FFI, or Club change.

ADR 0025 gave the Gradient an ordered dither and deliberately "only the three Bayer families". Bayer
is the pixel-art default but not the only look artists reach for: blue noise reads as grain-free
tone, clustered dots read as print halftone, lines and hatching are a drawing idiom of their own, and
noise dithers break the regular lattice. Every one of these is, like Bayer, a *threshold at a canvas
coordinate*: the engine's dither already compares the eased blend fraction against a matrix entry and
emits exactly one of the two bounding stop colors, so adding families is adding threshold functions,
not a second fill model.

**Decisions.**

- **One closed form for every family.** `DitherKind::threshold(x, y)` and `levels()` are the whole
  contract; `gradient_sample_sorted` compares `floor(local · levels) > threshold(cx, cy)` exactly as
  before. The fill, its draft preview, the probe oracle, and a journal replay evaluate the same
  expression, and every family keeps ADR 0025's guarantees: two stop colors per pixel (alpha
  included), smoothstep honored, anchored to the canvas origin, the global pattern never read.
- **Error diffusion is out.** Floyd–Steinberg and Atkinson are sequential (scan order, region shape)
  and would break the closed form, the oracle, and the per-pixel preview. Not a threshold matrix, not
  a Gradient dither.
- **Blue noise is a shipped 64×64 void-and-cluster table** (Ulichney 1993, σ = 1.9, a fixed seed),
  generated offline by `tools/dither/gen_tables.py` with numpy and committed as
  `crates/engine/src/tool/dither_tables.g.rs` — the engine stays dependency-free and the bytes are
  frozen by an FNV checksum test. The 4×4 and 8×8 halftones are generated by the same script (cells
  ranked by distance from the tile center, ties by angle; the 4×4 is the textbook clustered-dot
  matrix). Regenerating changes nothing unless the script changes.
- **Lines fill in by bit-reversed rank**, rows (or columns, or `x + y` classes) in the order
  0, n/2, n/4, 3n/4, …, and along each line by the same rank on the other axis: `n²` steps, and at
  50 % exactly every other line is solid. Periods 2/4/8 for horizontal and vertical, 4/8 for the
  diagonal.
- **The noises are integer-exact.** White noise is `util::hash_xy` under a fixed seed (the top byte
  of the hash); interleaved gradient noise is Jimenez's formula in 0.32 fixed point (the three
  constants times 2³², products wrapped). No floats, so no platform can fork a golden, and both
  are continuous across negative coordinates.
- **The wire stays backward compatible.** `SetGradientDither(0|2|4|8)` parses and means what it
  did; the new tokens are `blue`, `halftoneN`, `hlinesN`, `vlinesN`, `diagN`, `noise`, `ign`
  (case-insensitive; `off` and `bayerN` are accepted spellings). An old app replaying a new journal
  drops the unknown-token line under the replay line-skip, exactly as for any newer verb. The shell
  preference moved from a Bayer size (v1, int) to the token (v2, string), reading v1 for migration.
- **The shell previews with its own copy of the same math.** `gradient_dither.dart` mirrors
  `DitherKind`; `dither_tables.g.dart` is the generator's second output; the formula kinds are pinned
  to the same literal thresholds in both test suites. The row-1 swatch renders at 50 % density,
  which for every family is the density where its structure is most legible (solid alternate
  lines, half dots, the checker for Bayer); the page's strips (2026-09-16) ramp OFF→ON across
  their width through the engine's own pick rule (`floor(t · levels) > threshold`), so the user
  sees every density step the fill can produce, not one — the goal of the change.

Alternatives rejected:

- **A preview-tile FFI getter** (the engine renders the swatch). One source of truth, but the
  Patterns page and its widget tests would need the engine binary — Dart tests run without it by
  rule.
- **Reusing the Patterns catalog's tiles as gradient dithers.** A pattern tile is one bitmask, not a
  threshold ladder; a dither needs an ordering of every cell. The catalog's Bayer *levels* are
  slices of the ladder, not the ladder.
- **Runtime blue-noise generation.** Void-and-cluster is cheap once, but it needs a Gaussian
  (`det_exp` would do) and a few seconds on a phone at first use; a 8 KiB table is simpler, and the
  bytes are reviewable.
- **Density/contrast knobs per family.** A "dither strength" slider would be a second parameter on
  every family; nothing asked for it, and the stop positions already shape the ramp.

**Consequences.** New tokens `SetGradientDither` must parse forever. `DitherKind::ALL` is the
authoritative list; the shell's `DitherKind.all` mirrors it in the same order. Eight new hash pins in
`crates/engine/tests/patterns.rs` freeze the tables, the ranks, the seed, and the fixed-point
constants; a moved pin IS the bug. `docs/patterns/DESIGN.md` records the oracle's canvas-rect fix.
The `assert.gradient` limitation noted there is closed.
