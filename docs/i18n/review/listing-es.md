# Store listing review: Spanish (es-419)

| field | severity | problem | current | proposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| fullDescription | medium | The adjective sits after "capas", so the phrase is clumsy and can be misread as the layers being editable. "La fuente original" sounds technical, and "fuente" can also mean a font. | Los artistas pueden adjuntar su archivo con capas editable, para que partas de la fuente original | Los artistas pueden adjuntar su archivo editable con capas, para que partas del original |
| fullDescription | medium | "El arte de un reproductor" reads as the player's own artistry and does not use the glossary's "obra" for artwork. The intended meaning is the artwork a player shows. | El arte de un reproductor se actualiza en vivo desde el Club | Las obras que muestra un reproductor se actualizan en vivo desde el Club |
| fullDescription | low | "Integrado" at the end of a long noun phrase reads like a word-for-word translation of "built in". | Makapix Club es una comunidad de pixel art con un editor completo de pixel art animado integrado. | Makapix Club es una comunidad de pixel art que incluye un editor completo de pixel art animado. |

All three fields are within their length limits (title 24/30, shortDescription 74/80, fullDescription well under 4,000). The terms match the glossary: obra, publicar, feed, remix, capa, fotograma, lienzo, reproductor, iniciar sesión, and tú throughout. The title leaves out the second "Club" of the English, which is a reasonable choice given the 30-character limit.

**Verdict:** a good, natural translation in neutral Spanish that is ready to publish once the two medium items are fixed. Everything else reads like native store copy.
