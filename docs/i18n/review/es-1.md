## Systemic notes

1. **Promote / featured / Recommended do not line up.** The glossary renders promote as "destacar / quitar de destacados" and featured as "destacados", but the feed that promotion feeds is "Recomendados" (feedRecommended). The moderator menu and the notification therefore name a list the user cannot find. Either render promote as "recomendar / quitar de recomendados" (and notifPromoted as "Tu publicación se añadió a Recomendados"), or rename the feed; the first is the smaller change. This is the one glossary term I would change.
2. **The text leans peninsular.** Ordenador, móvil, pulsar ("mantén pulsado"), caducar, comprobar, "prueba a + infinitive". Each has a neutral counterpart (escritorio, teléfono, presionar, expirar, revisar / verificar, "intenta"). "Introduce", "gestionar", "añadir", and "ajustes" are also more common in Spain but are fully standard everywhere and can stay. Whatever is decided for tap/press verbs should be applied in parts 2 and 3 as well.
3. **Past tense is mixed in sibling messages.** Present perfect ("Tu sesión ha caducado", "Has bloqueado", "Has recibido", "hemos enviado") alternates with the simple past ("Se guardó", "Se desvinculó", "te otorgó", "Enviamos", "Iniciaste sesión"). Both are correct; pick one for toasts and notices. "Enviamos" in particular is ambiguous with the present tense.
4. **Hashtag vs. etiqueta.** The glossary term is "hashtag"; "etiqueta" slips in at modTagsAddLabel and hashtagFeedEmpty. The verb "etiquetar" (modTagsPostGone, artworkTaggedByMod) is fine, since "hashtag" has no verb.
5. **Generic masculine for people.** "invitado", "moderador", "propietario", "bloqueado", "desbloquearlo" follow the generic masculine. That is acceptable and standard; where a neutral wording is free (the block toasts) it is worth taking, as the translation already does in welcomeTitle and fieldTooLong.
6. **What is consistently right.** Placeholders and ICU plural/select branches are intact in all 550 messages; quotation marks are «…» throughout; opening ¿ is always present; the ellipsis is the single character; no added exclamation marks; "tú" is used without exception; glossary terms (obra, publicación, fotograma, capa, reproductor, nombre de usuario, denunciar, hashtags supervisados, archivo con capas) are applied consistently.
