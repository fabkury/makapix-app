# Store listing review: Simplified Chinese (zh-CN)

| field | severity | problem | current | proposed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shortDescription | low | "对……进行再创作" is a slightly heavy, translated construction for a store tagline; the verb can take the object directly. | 创作像素动画，分享作品，并对社区作品进行再创作。 | 创作像素动画，分享作品，再创作社区作品。 |
| fullDescription | low | "分享的作品" with no agent can read as "works you shared"; the English means other people's shared artworks. | 再创作：在编辑器中打开分享的作品，加入你的想法，发布你的版本 | 再创作：在编辑器中打开他人分享的作品，加入你的想法，发布你的版本 |
| fullDescription | low | "充足" (ample) is a weak stand-in for "deep", and "per-frame" (each frame keeps its own history) is only implied. | 每帧都有充足的撤销历史 | 每帧独立的多级撤销历史 |
| fullDescription | low | All-caps "MAKAPIX" copies the English heading style; Chinese headings have no capitals to match, and the brand is written "Makapix" everywhere else. | MAKAPIX 播放器 | Makapix 播放器 |

The translation is accurate, natural, and well suited to a store listing. Glossary terms are used consistently (动态, 关注, 回应, 评论, 发布, 再创作, 作品, 图层文件, 播放器, 账号, 登录, 画布, 帧, 图层, 选区, 调色板, 撤销, 导入/导出). Half-width spacing around Latin words and numbers, full-width punctuation, and 你 are correct throughout. Brand names and file formats are unchanged, and all three fields are well within the length limits. Only polish items remain; the listing can ship as is.
