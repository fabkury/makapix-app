# Chinese (Simplified and Traditional) pixel fonts that could be bundled in a pixel-art editor (as of 2026-11-26)

Method note: most facts below were read directly from each project's README / LICENSE on GitHub (raw files fetched 2026-11-26), plus the GitHub repository search API (stars, SPDX license, last push date). "Last push" dates are GitHub `pushed_at` values. Where a fact comes only from a search-engine summary of a page and not from the page itself, it is marked "(search summary, not read directly)". Anything not confirmed is in a Gaps list.

Shorthand: SC = Simplified Chinese, TC = Traditional Chinese, OFL = SIL Open Font License 1.1, RFN = Reserved Font Name.

---

## Key Question 1: Which Chinese pixel fonts exist under licenses that clearly permit bundling in an app, converting the format, and redistributing the converted data in a public repo?

### Takeaway
There is a much larger OFL-licensed pool than the three Fusion Pixel sizes: about 20 usable candidates, and the biggest find is a wave of 2025-2026 OFL fonts that add Chinese to Japanese OFL pixel fonts (the yzdnn series at 16/12/10/8 px in Song, Fangsong, round, reverse-contrast and decorative styles; Z Labs at 12 and 16 px; FashionBitmap16), which gives real style variety at 16 px. Ark Pixel 16 px is no longer an option: it was deprecated and removed after the 2026.09.01 build, and its own authors point to Unifont instead.

- **Zpix 最像素** — already rejected; a search summary of its page gives ¥7,000 per commercial product, free for personal and education projects — [SolidZORO/zpix-pixel-font](https://github.com/SolidZORO/zpix-pixel-font)
- **Pix32 三二像素体** (32comic) — 12 px (11 px + 1 px padding), 13,060 Hanzi (GB2312 + Big5) plus EUC-JP; TTF/OTF/WOFF/WOFF2. "for Commercial/Business Product (Multiple Product, include Software, App, web app/site, Game import) USD $200" (Chinese text: RMB ¥799); free for education and personal products. No open license; no statement on modification or redistribution of converted data. Last push 2026-10-10 — [32comic/Pix32](https://github.com/32comic/Pix32)

#### G. Fonts with unclear or disputed rights (see Key Question 5 for details)
HZK12/16 (UCDOS) and everything built on it: Vonwaon Bitmap 凤凰点阵体, IPix, mplus_hzk_12, TakWolf's hzk-pixel-font; Guanzhi 观致 8px (FontChinese7x7); Zfull; "xiaodianzhen" 小点阵 8px; Windows XP extracted bitmaps.

### Inferences
- For the stated pipeline (convert to a custom bitmap format, commit converted data to a public repo, ship in the app), OFL 1.1 is the cleanest fit: its own definitions say a "Modified Version" includes one made "by changing formats or by porting the Font Software to a new environment", and its preamble allows fonts and derivatives to be "bundled, embedded, redistributed and/or sold with any software provided that any reserved names are not used by derivative works" (text as reproduced in [MuzaiPixel LICENSE.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DWNfonts/MuzaiPixel/main/LICENSE.md)). The practical obligations are: keep the copyright notice and OFL text with the converted data, keep the converted data under OFL, and do not present the converted file under a Reserved Font Name as if it were the original. How the in-app picker may label a converted font whose name is reserved is a judgment call the existing Fusion Pixel integration has presumably already made; the same answer applies to every OFL font here.
- Best-value additions by gap filled, judging only from licenses and stated coverage: (1) 16 px Song: ChangBanDianSong 16; (2) 12 px Song: ChangBanDianSong 12; (3) 16 px rounded: QiuYeYuanTi 16 or Z Labs RoundPix 16px; (4) 12 px rounded: Z Labs RoundPix 12px (full 通用规范汉字表); (5) 16 px Fangsong/handwritten: RenOuFangSong 16; (6) 16 px display: ShanWeiZhouHei 16 (SC) / FashionBitmap16 (TC), BaDingShiWeiTi 16; (7) hand-drawn bold at 10 px: BoutiqueBitmap9x9 Bold; (8) narrow 8x12: MuzaiPixel; (9) tiny 6x8: XiuZhenXiangSuTi.
- Z Labs Pixel 12px is a second, independent 12 px gothic with complete GB2312 + 通用规范汉字表 + Big5-common coverage, but stylistically it is "more plain Hei" at a size Fusion already covers, so it adds little variety.
- Cubic 11 and Boutique 9x9/7x7 are redundant for TC text at 12/10/8 px only in the sense that Fusion borrows their glyphs to fill gaps; as standalone fonts they have their own Latin, punctuation and overall texture, and Boutique 9x9's Bold is not in Fusion at all.
- Sizes 7, 9, 11 px in the brief are mostly naming differences: "7x7" = 8 px cell, "9x9" = 10 px cell, "11x11" = 12 px cell. No clean free Chinese font was found at true 14, 20 or 24 px except GPL WenQuanYi (13/14/15 px), the CC-BY-SA Taipei 24, and the unverified "Lyusung 24px".
- Many of the strongest candidates are explicitly unfinished (RenOuFangSong, QiuYeYuanTi, BaDingShiWeiTi, ShanWeiZhouHei, ConcisePixel, RoundPix 16). A Text tool that ships them needs a visible missing-glyph behavior and a plan to re-import newer releases.

### Gaps

- "12×16莫妮卡圆像素" by 綿雲飴里 (a rounded 12x16 pixel font recommended in both Z Labs RoundPix READMEs, maoken entry 25830) — the maoken page timed out; license and coverage unverified.
- DWNfonts' QuanPixel (8x8, described as OFL), Fonsung/Chusung/Lyusung/CLFN (16/24 px Song-named bitmap fonts) and PoxiaoPixel: only one-line descriptions on the itch profile were seen; their READMEs/licenses returned 404 at the guessed paths. These could be additional Song-style 16/24 px candidates but are unverified, and their glyph sources are unknown.
- Nightgazer Bitmap's five exact pixel sizes, and Uranus Pixel's pixel size (the brief says 11 px; the README does not state a size).
- ChillBitmap's file formats, and what "Author's Statement" covers for the Guanzhi-derived glyphs in the 7px style.

## Key Question 2: For each, what does the license ACTUALLY say?

### Takeaway
The OFL group is unambiguous and the authors' own plain-language summaries explicitly cover commercial use, bundling in software, modification and redistribution; the ambiguous ones are the GPL family (the "embedding exception" speaks only about documents, and WenQuanYi's README asks commercial users to contact the maintainer), the CC0 label on Vonwaon (the upstream glyphs are not the author's to dedicate), and ChillBitmap 7px (contains glyphs of unknown ownership).

### Cited Findings
- **OFL, authors' own summaries.** Cubic 11, BoutiqueBitmap 9x9/7x7 and FashionBitmap16 use the same wording: "任何人可以無償使用此字型，包含商用。無須告知原作者" (anyone may use free of charge including commercially, no need to notify the author); "您可自由傳送、分享此字型，或與其他軟體綑綁發行、銷售。捆包中必須同時包含授權文件檔（OFL.txt）" (may share, or bundle and sell with other software; the bundle must include OFL.txt); "您可自由改造、衍生此字型並公開。修改後的字型必須同樣以 SIL OFL 進行發布，勿使用字型的保留名稱" (may modify and publish derivatives, which must be OFL and must not use the reserved names); "禁止單獨出售字型檔" (selling the font file by itself is prohibited) — [Cubic-11](https://github.com/ACh-K/Cubic-11), [BoutiqueBitmap9x9](https://github.com/scott0107000/BoutiqueBitmap9x9), [FashionBitmap16](https://github.com/scott0107000/FashionBitmap16)
- **OFL text on format conversion.** "Modified Version refers to any derivative made by adding to, deleting, or substituting -- in part or in whole -- any of the components of the Original Version, by changing formats or by porting the Font Software to a new environment"; "The requirement for fonts to remain under this license does not apply to any document created using the fonts or their derivatives" — [OFL text in MuzaiPixel LICENSE.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DWNfonts/MuzaiPixel/main/LICENSE.md)
- **Reserved Font Names declared** (each must not be used for a modified version): "Cubic", "俐方體"; "BoutiqueBitmap", "精品點陣體", "精品点阵体"; "FashionBitmap16", "流行點陣體16", "流行点阵体16"; "Z工坊", "Z Labs"; "MuzaiPixel"; "Ark Pixel Inherited"; and for each yzdnn font its Chinese and romanized name (e.g. "长坂点宋 16", "ChangBanDianSong 16") — respective READMEs linked in Key Question 1
- **Z Labs** spells out embedding: you may "在软件/设备中嵌入该字体软件" (embed the font software in software/devices) and "自行修改并制作衍生字体" (modify and make derivative fonts); conditions: not sold alone for profit, keep copyright and license info on distribution, derivatives keep OFL-1.1 and drop the reserved names, "不得将该字体软件用于非法用途" (no unlawful use — an extra sentence that is not part of OFL itself) — [ZLabs-RoundPix-16px](https://github.com/Astro-2539/ZLabs-RoundPix-16px)
- **Z Labs author's warning about resale**: many open and free-commercial fonts including this one are being resold by shops on Taobao and Xiaohongshu, which "严重违反了 OFL 1.1 中对字体售卖行为的限制条款" (seriously violates OFL's restriction on selling fonts) — [ZLabs-RoundPix-16px](https://github.com/Astro-2539/ZLabs-RoundPix-16px)
- **MuzaiPixel FAQ**: "虽然我们不用 WTFPL，但是您几乎可以 'DO WHAT THE FUCK YOU WANT TO'"; the author asks (not requires) that game makers send screenshots — [MuzaiPixel docs/faq.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DWNfonts/MuzaiPixel/main/docs/faq.md)
- **XiaoyaPixel-Classic (non-OFL, permissive custom)**: "也可以发布包含 .ttf 文件的游戏版本并用于商业用途。也可以进行加工、修改和再分发，但不放弃字体的版权 ... 无论字体是否经过加工或修改，都不能声称自己是字体的作者。（因此，出售字体本身或任何类似行为都是非法的，因此是被禁止的）。您可以注明或不注明使用字体的来源或出处" — and "这些条款和条件如有变更，恕不另行通知" (terms may change without notice) — [LICENSE](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DWNfonts/XiaoyaPixel-Classic/main/LICENSE)
- **WenQuanYi Bitmap Song (GPL v2 + exception)**: "You are free to copy, distribute, and/or modify this font as long as you pass the freedoms to the users of the derived work." Exception text: "As a special exception, if you create a document which uses this font, and embed this font or unaltered portions of this font into the document, this font does not by itself cause the resulting document to be covered by the GNU General Public License ... If you modify this font, you may extend this exception to your version of the font, but you are not obligated to do so." Separately: "For commercial use, please contact the project maintainer (Qianqian Fang) or consult copyright law firms to make sure your plan is compliant with the font licenses. Commercial licenses are also available upon request." — [WenQuanYi README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AmusementClub/WenQuanYi-Bitmap-Song-TTF/master/Original%20Fonts/README)
- **Unifont**: dual-licensed OFL 1.1 / GPLv2+ with font embedding exception since 13.0.04; "Derivative variants of Unifont are permitted under the terms of the dual license"; WenQuanYi's Qianqian Fang granted OFL for the WenQuanYi glyphs in Unifont in October 2020 — [unifoundry.com/unifont](https://unifoundry.com/unifont/index.html)
- **ChillBitmap** README itself notes that the 16px CJK glyphs of WenQuanYi 16px "与Unifont中文部分基本一致" (are basically identical to Unifont's Chinese part) — [Warren2060/ChillBitmap](https://github.com/Warren2060/ChillBitmap)
- **Vonwaon Bitmap 凤凰点阵体**: "凤凰点阵体是免费的中文像素字体，使用 CC0 1.0 协议发布，属于公有领域" (free Chinese pixel font, released under CC0 1.0, public domain); glyphs based on aguegu/BitmapFont. In comments the author concedes that HZK/ASC-based projects carry copyright risk in the strict sense, says the GPL in the upstream repo covers only its C++ parser, and advises users with strict requirements to avoid HZK/ASC-based projects — [timothyqiu.itch.io/vonwaon-bitmap](https://timothyqiu.itch.io/vonwaon-bitmap)
- **Taipei Fonts**: "CC-BY SA 4.0" (share-alike, attribution) — [itch.io](https://diaowinner.itch.io/taipei-fonts)
- **Pix32**: priced commercial license only (USD $200 / RMB ¥799 for multiple commercial products including app embedding); nothing about modification or redistribution — [32comic/Pix32](https://github.com/32comic/Pix32)
- **mplus_hzk_12**: GitHub license "Unlicense", but the author's own README conditions it: "如果hzk 真的没有版权, mplus_hzk_12 字体也是没有任何版权" (IF HZK really has no copyright, then this font has none either); HZK12 is "据说没有版权" (said to have no copyright) — [py-zhao/mplus_hzk_12](https://github.com/py-zhao/mplus_hzk_12)

### Inferences
- GPL fonts and this app: converting WenQuanYi-family BDF/PCF data to a custom format creates a modified version that must stay GPL v2 and be offered with its source form; a public repo containing the converter and the original BDF would meet the source-offer part. The open questions are (a) whether shipping GPL font data inside a non-GPL app binary is "mere aggregation" (the exception only addresses documents, not programs) and (b) whether pixels stamped into a user's artwork count as a "document" embedding "unaltered portions". The second seems comfortably within the spirit of the exception; the first is the real risk, and App Store distribution of GPL material is a known friction point. This is my reading, not a cited legal conclusion.
- The Unifont dual license offers a way around the GPL for 16 px only: WenQuanYi's 16 px glyphs are available under OFL through Unifont, so a 16 px "WenQuanYi-looking" Song is already obtainable as OFL (Unifont itself, or ChillBitmap 16px with its redesigned Latin). The 12/13/14/15 px WenQuanYi sizes have no OFL route that I found.
- "免费商用" (free commercial use) labels on aggregator sites are not sufficient by themselves; every recommended font above has an explicit OFL statement in its own repo, which does address modification and redistribution.
- XiaoyaPixel-Classic's custom terms do allow modification and redistribution, but the "may change without notice" clause and the lack of a standard license make it weaker than Z Labs RoundPix 12px, which derives from the same Japanese font and is plain OFL.

### Gaps
- I did not open each repo's actual OFL.txt / LICENSE file except Cubic 11's and MuzaiPixel's; for the rest the license is taken from the README statement and GitHub's SPDX detection (OFL-1.1 for the yzdnn 16/12/8 fonts and ChillBitmap; "NOASSERTION" for Cubic 11, Boutique, FashionBitmap16, ConcisePixel 10 and MuzaiPixel, where the README states OFL but the license file has extra preamble text).
- No primary source was found for whether a converted, renamed-internally bitmap blob shown in a UI under the original font's name violates an RFN; this needs the OFL FAQ or the authors' consent.
- WenQuanYi's wenq.org pages were not fetched; the license text comes from the release README mirrored in a GitHub repo.

## Key Question 3: Which ones offer a visibly different style from plain Hei/gothic?

### Takeaway
Style variety exists almost only at 16 px (plus Song at 12 px and rounded at 12 px), and nearly all of it comes from Chinese extensions of the Japanese KH Dot Font Series; no free pixel Kai (regular script) font with a clean license was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Song / Ming (serif)**: ChangBanDianSong 16 and 12 ("点阵粗宋体", bold bitmap Song) — [16](https://github.com/yzdnn/ChangBanDianSong-16), [12](https://github.com/yzdnn/ChangBanDianSong-12); WenQuanYi Bitmap Song 12-16 px (GPL) — [README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AmusementClub/WenQuanYi-Bitmap-Song-TTF/master/Original%20Fonts/README); Unifont 16 px, which Ark Pixel's authors describe as "16px 宋体" (16 px Song) — [16px archive README](https://github.com/pixel-font-studio/ark-pixel-font-16px-glyphs-archive)
- **Fangsong / handwritten-leaning**: RenOuFangSong 16 — the source font removed all right-falling diagonal strokes and replaced them with dots, and is suggested "作为装饰性手写体使用" (as a decorative handwriting face) — [yzdnn/RenOuFangSong-16](https://github.com/yzdnn/RenOuFangSong-16)
- **Rounded (Yuan)**: QiuYeYuanTi 16 — [repo](https://github.com/yzdnn/QiuYeYuanTi-16); Z Labs RoundPix 16px and 12px — [16](https://github.com/Astro-2539/ZLabs-RoundPix-16px), [12](https://github.com/Astro-2539/ZLabs-RoundPix-12px); XiaoyaPixel 12 px — [repo](https://github.com/DWNfonts/XiaoyaPixel-Classic); Jelly Pixel 12 px (not usable yet) — [repo](https://github.com/TakWolf/jelly-pixel-font)
- **Reverse-contrast / heavy display**: ShanWeiZhouHei 16 (SC) — "横画比竖画更粗 ... 适合在需要厚重像素风字体的场景" (horizontals thicker than verticals; for heavy pixel text) — [repo](https://github.com/yzdnn/ShanWeiZhouHei-16); FashionBitmap16 (TC) — [repo](https://github.com/scott0107000/FashionBitmap16)
- **Decorative / sci-fi signage**: BaDingShiWeiTi 16 — [repo](https://github.com/yzdnn/BaDingShiWeiTi-16)
- **Bold**: BoutiqueBitmap9x9 Bold (designed weight, 10 px cell) — [repo](https://github.com/scott0107000/BoutiqueBitmap9x9); Uranus Pixel (non-clogging bold, GPL) — [repo](https://github.com/scott0107000/Uranus-Pixel); Nightgazer Bitmap bold weights (GPL) — [repo](https://github.com/scott0107000/Nightgazer-Bitmap); Fusion Bold (algorithmic, not recommended by its author) — [repo](https://github.com/pixel-font-studio/fusion-bold-pixel-font)
- **Japanese-vendor gothic flavor** (still gothic, but not the "Song-Hei hybrid" of GB bitmaps): ZhengGeDianHei 16 — [repo](https://github.com/yzdnn/ZhengGeDianHei-16)
- **Unusual proportions**: MuzaiPixel 8x12 narrow — [repo](https://github.com/DWNfonts/MuzaiPixel); Hexin 12x8 wide — [repo](https://github.com/DWNfonts/Hexin-Pixel-Font); XiuZhenXiangSuTi 6x8 — [repo](https://github.com/yzdnn/XiuZhenXiangSuTi)
- **Dot-matrix / LED-style transforms**: Dotted Chinese Pixel fonts (GPL 2.0, generated styles from WenQuanYi) — [repo](https://github.com/wixette/dotted-chinese-fonts)
- **Kai exists only in legacy, rights-encumbered form**: a specimen archive documents "Mac OS / Ikarus Kai 12×12", "延伸震汉 V3.00 ... 24×24 简楷" and "IBM PC DOS 三套24点：楷体、黑体和仿宋" as historical ROM/system fonts, and states "字体使用与再分发按原来源许可执行" (use and redistribution follow the original sources' licenses) — [legendtired/chinese-bitmap-specimens](https://github.com/legendtired/chinese-bitmap-specimens)
- **Not pixel fonts at a native size** (excluded): Warren2060's ChillMatrix 寒蝉矩阵体 is designed on a 1000-unit height with a 50-unit grid ("可以看成是20px点阵风格字体" but with stroke width 150 units), and ChillMatrixBlack likewise "基于1000像素的高度以內设计" — creative pixel-style vector faces rather than a fixed-size bitmap — [ChillMatrix](https://github.com/Warren2060/ChillMatrix), [ChillMatrixBlack](https://github.com/Warren2060/ChillMatrixBlack)

### Inferences
- A balanced menu extension would be: 16 px Song (ChangBanDianSong 16), 16 px rounded (QiuYeYuanTi 16), 16 px Fangsong (RenOuFangSong 16), one 16 px display face (ShanWeiZhouHei 16 + FashionBitmap16 as its TC counterpart), 12 px Song (ChangBanDianSong 12), 12 px rounded (Z Labs RoundPix 12px), 10 px Bold (Boutique 9x9 Bold). All OFL.
- Because the yzdnn fonts extend Japanese sources, they should also render kana and Japanese kanji from the original, which may let one font entry serve Japanese and Chinese; glyph-form differences between regions would need checking.
- ChillMatrix at a 20-row grid could in principle be rasterized exactly at 20 px, but its strokes are 3 grid units wide by design and spacing is tuned in units, so it does not meet the "exactly on a pixel grid at a stated size" test without verification; its repo also shows no license on GitHub (SPDX none).

- No clean-licensed pixel Kai or Lishu Chinese font was found. BaDingShiWeiTi's source was labeled Lishu by its Japanese author but the Chinese adapter says the Hanzi do not read as Lishu.
- Whether ChillPixels ("4 styles. Free commercial use.") contains any Chinese — its README has no detail and GitHub shows no license — [Warren2060/ChillPixels](https://github.com/Warren2060/ChillPixels)

## Key Question 5: Any provenance concerns (fonts extracted from ROMs, operating systems, or commercial products)?

### Takeaway
Yes: everything descended from HZK12/HZK16 (Vonwaon, IPix, mplus_hzk_12, hzk-pixel-font) rests on glyphs that were extracted from DOS-era software and whose owner is disputed, regardless of the CC0 / Unlicense / "public domain" labels attached downstream; Guanzhi 8px, Zfull, "xiaodianzhen" and Windows XP extractions are likewise unlicensed, and ChillBitmap 7px admits to containing a few Guanzhi glyphs.

### Cited Findings
- **HZK (UCDOS) glyphs**: TakWolf's conversion project opens with "本字体并非公有领域，您在使用时，请务必谨慎考量并自负版权风险" (this font is not public domain; consider carefully and bear the copyright risk yourself). Glyphs come from aguegu/BitmapFont; that project's author said the data "可能来源于 UCDOS，或是早期的 WPS" (may come from UCDOS or early WPS); "其他网友考证，字模由香港 Compufont 制作" (other users' research: the glyphs were made by Compufont of Hong Kong). Offers 12 px and 16 px previews — [pixel-font-legacy/hzk-pixel-font](https://github.com/pixel-font-legacy/hzk-pixel-font)
- **Vonwaon Bitmap 凤凰点阵体** (Haoyu Qiu / timothyqiu) — 12 px and 16 px TTF; full version 7,543 glyphs including 6,763 Hanzi (GB2312), Lite 4,041 glyphs including 3,755 level-1 Hanzi; last update 1.02 on 2022-05-12; labeled CC0. Commenters point out the upstream author did not hold the copyright, that the glyphs were extracted from DOS fonts, and that one tracing attributes them to a Beijing company still in business; another asked why a GPL3-repo derivative is CC0. The author's reply: the GPL covers only the parser, the glyph data has no clear owner, and those with strict requirements should avoid HZK/ASC-based projects — [timothyqiu.itch.io/vonwaon-bitmap](https://timothyqiu.itch.io/vonwaon-bitmap)
- **IPix** (Sadi / purestudio) — based on HZK16 from aguegu/BitmapFont. A commenter warned "这个字体的上游版权有点问题，并不属于公共领域" (the upstream copyright is problematic, not public domain), citing BitmapFont issues 2 and 3 and a tracing to 北京中易中标电子信息技术有限公司 (Beijing Zhongyi Electronic); the author replied "感谢提醒指正，我简介更改下 ... 以为是早期字体之后公司倒闭了" (thanks, I will change the description; I had assumed the company had gone under). Another commenter added that Zhongyi has not closed and still runs font, including bitmap-font, business — [purestudio.itch.io/ipix](https://purestudio.itch.io/ipix)
- **Conflicting attributions for HZK**: Compufont (Hong Kong) per the hzk-pixel-font README versus Zhongyi (Beijing) per itch commenters; Vonwaon's author doubts the Zhongyi tracing because the glyphs predate Windows 3.1; one commenter says HZK "appears to be a national standard" — [hzk-pixel-font](https://github.com/pixel-font-legacy/hzk-pixel-font), [Vonwaon comments](https://timothyqiu.itch.io/vonwaon-bitmap)
- **mplus_hzk_12** — M+ Latin/Japanese plus HZK12 for GB2312; freedom explicitly conditional on HZK being uncopyrighted — [py-zhao/mplus_hzk_12](https://github.com/py-zhao/mplus_hzk_12)
- **Guanzhi 观致 8px (FontChinese7x7, Angelic47)** — 7x7 Chinese glyphs; "具体的版权信息难以知晓" (copyright details hard to determine); the font file's metadata names 北京中易中标电子信息技术有限公司 as copyright holder and claims it was "supplemented on the basis of the Japanese font Misaki"; published "处于学习和研究的目的" (for study and research), with a takedown offer; "除字体文件版权无法知晓外, 代码均为MIT License ... 原字体文件的作者依旧保留对该字体文件的版权" (code is MIT; the original font's author retains copyright). Includes a community-made `JiZhi-bitmap-8.bdf`. The README's motivation mentions an 8x8 font that is paid and "明确说明不得以任何形式嵌入到软件当中" (explicitly may not be embedded in software in any form) — [Angelic47/FontChinese7x7](https://github.com/Angelic47/FontChinese7x7)
- **ChillBitmap 7px contains Guanzhi glyphs**: "融合部分{精品點陣體7×7}字形和极少一部分{Guanzhi 8px}的字形" with the source cited as a themex.net forum thread — [Warren2060/ChillBitmap](https://github.com/Warren2060/ChillBitmap)
- **Zfull** — "过去曾在网络流行的点阵字体" (a bitmap font once popular online), taken from the andot/zfull-for-yosemite repo, "作者以及版权不明" (author and copyright unknown), not public domain; original was an EBDT embedded-bitmap font, converted to one outline font per strike — [pixel-font-legacy/zfull-pixel-font](https://github.com/pixel-font-legacy/zfull-pixel-font)
- **"xiaodianzhen" 小点阵 8 px** — "来源不明，作者以及版权不明" (source, author and copyright unknown); "原始字体的版权声明可能是不正确的" (the original's copyright statement may be incorrect) — [pixel-font-legacy/xiaodianzhen-pixel-font](https://github.com/pixel-font-legacy/xiaodianzhen-pixel-font)
- **Windows XP bitmap strikes** — "原始字体提取自 Windows XP SP3 x86 官方镜像，版权归属于原相关制作公司" (extracted from the official XP SP3 image; copyright belongs to the original makers) — [pixel-font-legacy/winxp-pixel-font](https://github.com/pixel-font-legacy/winxp-pixel-font)
- **WenQuanYi's 16 px glyphs trace to a national standard**: "The 12pt bitmap glyphs between U3400-U9FA5 were derived from Chinese national standard GB19966-2005", listed as "(mandatory)", and the font builds on firefly's bitmap font — [WenQuanYi README](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AmusementClub/WenQuanYi-Bitmap-Song-TTF/master/Original%20Fonts/README)
- **Legacy ROM/system fonts** (Nokia, Samsung, Sony PSP, China PS2, Windows 3.x SimSun/MingLi, IBM PC DOS 24-dot Kai/Hei/Fangsong, Stone MS-2401H 32-dot sets, 文曲星 devices, Casio Ex-word, etc.) are catalogued by the specimen archive, which deliberately publishes only fixed sample images and offline extraction methods — "原字体、ROM 和研究输入放在被忽略的 local-research/，不随网站发布" (original fonts, ROMs and research inputs are not published) — [legendtired/chinese-bitmap-specimens](https://github.com/legendtired/chinese-bitmap-specimens)
- **Taipei Fonts** come from X.org's taipei16/24.bdf and are relicensed by the converter as CC-BY-SA 4.0 — [itch.io](https://diaowinner.itch.io/taipei-fonts)
- **Fusion Poke Pixel** states it has no relationship with the Pokémon rights holders and that all glyphs are original or derived from open fonts — [repo](https://github.com/pixel-font-studio/fusion-poke-pixel-font)

### Inferences
- Avoid the whole HZK lineage for a store-distributed app with a public repo: the people who know these glyphs best (TakWolf, the Vonwaon and IPix authors) all now warn against treating them as public domain, and at least one named candidate owner is an operating company in the bitmap-font business.
- ChillBitmap 7px should be treated as tainted by its small Guanzhi component unless the author can identify and remove those glyphs; ChillBitmap 16px (Unifont-based) does not have this problem.
- The Japanese-derived OFL fonts have a clean chain as far as the READMEs state: an OFL (or M+-licensed) Japanese original by a named author plus newly drawn Chinese glyphs by a named adapter. The residual risk is whether adapters drew new Hanzi themselves or copied shapes from other bitmap fonts; MuzaiPixel/XiaoyaPixel/Hexin openly say some glyphs are auto-generated by tools.
- The X11 taipei fonts' original terms were not checked, so the CC-BY-SA relabel on Taipei Fonts should not be relied on without reading the BDF headers.
- National-standard bitmap fonts (GB 5199, GB 5007, GB 6345, GB 19966 and similar) are a separate question from HZK: the fact that WenQuanYi derived glyphs from GB 19966-2005 and ships them as GPL, and Unifont as OFL, shows the free-software community has treated that derivation as acceptable, but I found no primary statement of the standards' own usage terms.

- HZK14/24/32/40/48, ETen 倚天 15/16/24 px, Kingsoft / 中文之星-era fonts, Zfull-GB vs Zfull-BIG5 specifics, and the X11 CJK fonts (gb16fs, gb24st, cclib, Arphic) were not individually researched.
