Shinonome, Ayu and Kappa bitmap fonts

Shinonome Mincho 12 and 14, Shinonome Marumoji 12, Shinonome Gothic 16, Ayu Mincho 18 and Kappa 20
are public-domain Japanese bitmap fonts. The app's data was converted from the TrueType builds
published as JF Dot fonts by Jikasei Font Kobo (http://jikasei.me/font/jf-dotfont/), read from
the bitmaps embedded at each font's native size. The glyphs are unchanged.
